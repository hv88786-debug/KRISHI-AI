🌱 Built for Bharat's Farmers

We are four CSE students from Government Engineering College, Ajmer — passionate about using technology to solve real problems for Indian farmers.

Our Mission
Technology that reaches every field
India has 140 million farming families. Most lack access to timely information, fair markets, and modern tools. कृषि AI🌾 was born from a simple belief — that a first-year engineering student should be able to build something that helps a farmer earn better.
We started with a hackathon project and built a complete ecosystem: IoT sensors, AI disease detection, live market prices, government scheme matching, and voice assistance — all in one platform.

Our Journey
From idea to award-winning platform
🌱
Project Inception — कृषि AI🌾 Idea Born
January 2026
Started as a classroom discussion about precision farming. Team Alpha Coders decided to build a real, working smart farming system for the upcoming hackathon season.
🔧
Hardware Prototype — ESP32 + Sensors
February 2026
Built first prototype with ESP32, TDS sensor, turbidity, NTC thermistor, and salinity copper strip sensor. Struggled with ADC calibration and EC derivation — solved it!
