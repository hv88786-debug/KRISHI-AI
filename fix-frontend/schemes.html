❓ FAQs
Scheme FAQs
💰
PM Kisan — How to apply?
▼
Step 1: Visit pmkisan.gov.in or your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).
Step 2: Submit Aadhaar card, land ownership records (Khatauni), and bank passbook.
Step 3: After verification, ₹2,000 is deposited in 3 installments every 4 months.

Helpline: 155261 / 1800115526 (Toll Free)
🧪
Soil Health Card — When do I get results?
▼
After submitting your soil sample at the local agriculture office, results are typically ready within 15–30 days. The card lists NPK levels, pH, and specific fertilizer recommendations for your soil. Cards are issued once every 2 years.
🌾
Fasal Bima — What crops are covered?
▼
Under PMFBY, the following crops are covered in Rajasthan: Wheat, Bajra, Maize, Mustard, Groundnut, Soybean, Onion, Tomato, Chilli, and more. Premium rates are 2% for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi. The remaining premium is borne by State and Central governments.
🏦
Kisan Credit Card — Eligibility?
▼
Any farmer who owns or leases agricultural land is eligible. Apply at your nearest nationalized bank or cooperative bank. Required documents: land records, Aadhaar, passport photo. Credit limit is based on crop cultivation cost + maintenance expenses. Interest rate: 4% per annum (with government subsidy).
