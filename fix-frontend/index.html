❓ Common Questions
Got questions?
📡
Do I need IoT hardware to use कृषि AI🌾?
▼
No! You can use all AI features — disease detection, crop recommendation, market prices, and scheme finder — without any hardware. IoT sensors (ESP32-based kit) are optional and enhance the experience with real-time soil data and automated irrigation. The kit costs ₹1,200–1,800 and is available through our partners.
🤖
How accurate is the AI disease detection?
▼
Our CNN model is trained on 50,000+ leaf images covering 38 diseases across wheat, rice, tomato, potato, and more. In validation tests, accuracy reached 98%. For best results: photograph a single affected leaf in good natural lighting, close-up (15–20cm distance).
☎️
Can farmers without smartphones use this?
▼
Yes! Our Voice AI Assistant is accessible by calling our toll-free number (1800-XXX-XXXX). The AI understands Hindi and Rajasthani, and can answer questions about crop health, mandi prices, government schemes, and irrigation advice — completely free, no internet needed.
💸
Is कृषि AI🌾 free?
▼
The core platform — disease detection, crop advisor, market prices, government schemes — is 100% free for all farmers. The IoT hardware kit is a one-time purchase. We are funded through government grants and social impact investors, ensuring that no farmer is priced out of smart farming technology.
